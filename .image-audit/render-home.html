WHY CHOOSE US

Why Dhanush Briyani?

Five promises behind every feast.

  1. 01

    Cooked with Casuarina Wood

    Our briyani is traditionally cooked using only Casuarina wood for an authentic firewood cooking experience.

  2. 02

    On-Time Catering

    We focus on timely preparation and catering service for every celebration.

  3. 03

    Fresh-Cut Meat

    No pre-cut meat. Meat is freshly cut for every cooking batch.

  4. 04

    Quality Ingredients

    Made with quality ingredients, crafted for great taste, and served with your satisfaction in mind.

  5. 05

    No Palm Oil. No Ajinomoto.

    We never use palm oil or Ajinomoto in our catering.
